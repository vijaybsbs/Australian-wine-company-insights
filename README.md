## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Australian Wine Company Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed using **Tableau** to analyze sales performance, product trends, customer behavior and key business performance indicators.

The project focuses on transforming business data into an interactive dashboard that allows users to explore performance trends, compare products and customers, monitor KPIs and identify business patterns.

The analytical workflow follows:

**Data Understanding → Data Analysis → KPI Development → Visualization → Dashboarding → Business Insights**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The objective is to create an interactive business intelligence solution that helps management understand overall business performance and answer practical questions around:

- Sales performance
- Revenue trends
- Product performance
- Customer contribution
- KPI performance
- Business trends
- Performance comparison

The dashboard is designed to make business performance easier to monitor and support **data-driven decision-making**.

---

## ⚠️ Analytical Limitations

1. The dashboard represents the data available in the project dataset and should be interpreted within its underlying data scope.
2. Dashboard-level patterns indicate relationships and trends but do not automatically establish causation.
3. Customer analysis represents the available customer-level data and should not be interpreted as complete customer lifetime value unless explicitly calculated.
4. Business recommendations should be validated using current operational and financial data before implementation.
